# cabextract mixed CAB fixture

`cabextract-mixed.cab.b64` is a Base64 representation of
`test/cabs/mixed.cab` from the cabextract 1.11 source distribution. It is an
inert 379-byte test cabinet containing three short text files compressed with
MSZIP, LZX, and Quantum respectively.

executed and contains no malware or private indicators.
